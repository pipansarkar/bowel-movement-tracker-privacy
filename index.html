Privacy Policy

App: Bowel Movement Tracker (com.focustech.bowelmovementtracker)
Effective date: 4 October 2026

Summary

Bowel Movement Tracker stores everything you log only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. The app does not have internet access.

Information you enter

The app lets you record bowel-movement details such as Bristol stool type, size, colour, how you felt, time on the toilet, water intake, symptoms/traits and free-text notes. This information is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Information we collect automatically

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and it does not request the internet permission.

Exporting your data

If you choose to export your entries (as a CSV file), the app creates the file and opens your device's share sheet. Where the file goes next is entirely your choice, and is then governed by the app or service you share it with.

Notifications

The app can show a local reminder if you have not logged an entry for a few days. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no data is sent anywhere to do this. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries in the app (swipe left on an entry). Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes all of your data. Because we never receive your data, we have no copy to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Medical disclaimer

This app is a personal log and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about any health concerns.
